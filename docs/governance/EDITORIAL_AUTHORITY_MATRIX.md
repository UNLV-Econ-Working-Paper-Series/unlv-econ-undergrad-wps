# Editorial Authority Matrix

Version: 1.0

Controlling charter: `PUBLICATION_GOVERNANCE.md`

All people named in the matrix must be non-conflicted for the action they perform. `Private record` means the evidence belongs in the private operations repository under the applicable NSHE/UNLV retention classification. Public notices must not disclose protected, unnecessary, or unsafe information.

## Decision-record minimum

For any vote or post-publication action, the private record must identify:

- paper identifier and version;
- action requested and initiator;
- evidence received;
- conflict disclosures and recusals;
- eligible decision-makers;
- verifier and findings;
- approval or vote;
- effective date and implementation actor;
- author/faculty-sponsor notifications;
- OAsis contact/response where applicable;
- public notice or reason no notice was lawful/safe;
- file and metadata before/after state; and
- next review date for a temporary action.

Operational templates remain blocked until the responsible records officer confirms the applicable NSHE/UNLV classification and the named role holders accept the charter.
