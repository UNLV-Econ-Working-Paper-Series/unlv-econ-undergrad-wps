# Economics Hub WordPress Migration Notice

Status: ready-to-post copy only. The old WordPress site has **not** been changed or verified as changed.

## Recommended notice

### The Undergraduate Economics Working Paper Series has moved

Current working papers, issue records, research fields, and citation tools are now available on the [UNLV Undergraduate Economics Working Paper Series website](https://econ-undergrad-wps.sites.unlv.edu/).

Permanent repository copies of current papers remain available through the [UNLV Libraries OAsis collection](https://oasis.library.unlv.edu/econ_ug_papers/).

Earlier Economics Hub records are being inventoried and verified. An older paper’s presence on this site does not mean that it has already been migrated, deposited in OAsis, cleared for republication, or fully audited for accessibility. Please use the [Series contact page](https://econ-undergrad-wps.sites.unlv.edu/contact/) to report a metadata problem, request an accessible copy, or ask about a historical record.

## Short banner version

## Placement instructions

1. Add the short banner immediately below the site header on the WordPress Working Paper Series landing page and on the Volume 1, Volume 2, and Volume 3 pages.
2. Add the full notice above the first historical paper listing on the Working Paper Series landing page.
3. Use the linked phrase “current Series website”; do not expose a raw URL as the only link text.
4. Preserve the existing historical pages and their original PDF URLs while the inventory, rights review, and accessibility work remain open. Do not replace them with silent redirects.
5. Do not upload historical PDFs to the new Series site as part of posting this notice.
6. After publishing, verify the notice while signed out on mobile and desktop, confirm all three links, and record the date and responsible editor in the private operations log.
